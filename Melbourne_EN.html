Great Ocean Road

Distance≈510 km
Driving≈7 hrs
Apostles1:00pm

Go straight to the Twelve Apostles on the fast inland highway (about 3 hours), arriving around 1pm. Explore the Port Campbell coast and Supercharge over a late lunch. Then drive the Great Ocean Road back east in the afternoon and evening, stopping at the sights on the way to the hotel.

⚠️ Heads-up today (7)▾
  • ⏰Long day: about 12½ hours, home around 10:30pm. Share the driving and take a break every 2 hours.
  • ↩️Heading east, the ocean is on the far side of the road, so turning into lookouts means crossing traffic. Indicate early and only pull in where there's a proper car park.
  • 🛑The road is narrow and bendy with sheer drops. Use the slow-vehicle turnouts to let faster traffic pass, and expect cars braking suddenly for photos.
  • 🦘The Apollo Bay → Lorne stretch runs into the evening. Kangaroos and wallabies come out at dusk (sunset is ~8:45pm), so slow down after 8pm.
  • 📶No phone reception for long stretches between Port Campbell, Lavers Hill and Apollo Bay. Download offline maps.
  • 🚌Tour buses crowd the Twelve Apostles 11am–3pm. Be patient at the lookouts. The coast gets quieter after 4pm.
  • 🪰Bush flies are common on warm days. A fly net or repellent helps.
⚡ Charging plan▾

Leave at 100% (charged last night at Box Hill). The inland drive to the Twelve Apostles is about 230 km, so you'll arrive with roughly 50% in a Long Range or 35% in an RWD. Colac Supercharger is on the way if you want a top-up during the coffee stop. The coast road home has no Superchargers, so charge to 90–100% at Port Campbell over lunch (4 stalls; it can queue in summer). The 300 km home along the coast is hilly. RWD drivers: route the final leg via Geelong (Corio, 24/7) for a 10–15 minute top-up.

Phillip Island eco-tour

Distance≈340 km
Driving≈4½ hrs
Penguins~8:45pm

A late start after New Year's Eve. Wildlife in the afternoon, then the Penguin Parade at sunset. Penguin Parade tickets sell out in summer, so book early. If you've booked a guided eco-tour, use its times instead.

⚠️ Heads-up today (5)▾
  • 📅New Year's Day is a public holiday: some cafés close and most add a 10–15% surcharge.
  • 🚗Holiday traffic onto the island is heavy in the afternoon and leaving after the parade. The San Remo bridge is the only way on and off.
  • 🧥It can be 30°C at lunch and 14°C with wind at the parade. Bring warm layers.
  • 📵Photos and video aren't allowed at the Penguin Parade, including phones.
  • 🦘Island roads after dark: wallabies, penguins, and Cape Barren geese near Churchill Island.
⚡ Charging plan▾

Leave at 90% or more. Phillip Island has no Supercharger. A Long Range Model Y completes the day without charging. If you have an RWD, or you're under 35% after the parade, stop at Dandenong Plaza (6 stalls, 24/7) on the way home; it's a short detour.

Trip essentials

🚦 Driving & safety in Victoria▾
  • 🚋
    Trams & hook turnsIn the CBD, some intersections make you turn right from the LEFT lane (a "hook turn"); signs show where. When a tram stops and its doors open, stop behind it — passengers step straight onto the road.
  • 💳
    TollsCityLink, EastLink and the West Gate Tunnel are cashless. Without an e-tag, buy a Linkt pass online within 3 days of travel, or choose "Avoid tolls" in Google Maps.
  • 📷
    Speed & phonesVictoria has many fixed, mobile and point-to-point cameras, with no tolerance to speak of. Holding a phone while driving costs a large fine and demerit points: mount it before you start.
  • 🦘
    WildlifeKangaroos and wallabies are most active at dawn and dusk, especially on the Great Ocean Road and Phillip Island. Slow down rather than swerve.
  • 🔥
    Fire dangerLate December can bring Total Fire Ban days. Check the VicEmergency app each morning, especially for the Dandenongs and the Otways. Some attractions change or close on extreme days.
  • ☀️
    Sun & weatherMelbourne can be 18°C or 40°C, sometimes on the same day. UV is extreme: hat, sunscreen and water. The coast and Phillip Island get cold and windy in the evening, so bring a jacket.
  • 🏖️
    BeachesOnly swim at patrolled beaches between the red and yellow flags. Don't swim at the Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge or Gibson Steps: strong rips.
  • 📶
    Phone coverageCoverage drops out in the Otways (Apollo Bay → Lavers Hill) and in parts of the Dandenongs. Download offline Google Maps for Victoria before you go.
  • 🆘
    EmergencyCall 000 for police, fire or ambulance. Tesla Roadside: 1800 646 952.
